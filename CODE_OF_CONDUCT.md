# Code of Conduct

Everyone who takes part in this project — in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions — is expected to follow this code.

## Our standards

- **Be respectful.** Critique code and decisions, not people.
- **Assume good intent**, and ask before concluding.
- **Be constructive in reviews.** Explain *why*, suggest an alternative, and separate "must fix" from "nice to have".
- **Be welcoming.** Write so that people of every experience level, background and language can follow. Questions are always welcome, including beginner ones and questions in Bahasa Indonesia.
- **Protect secrets.** Never post passwords, private keys, `.env` files or other people's infrastructure details.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer, [@ilramdhan](https://github.com/ilramdhan), through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and nobody will face retaliation for reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove or edit comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently block contributors who repeatedly or seriously break it.
